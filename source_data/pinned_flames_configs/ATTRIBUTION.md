# Source attribution

The copied FLAMES source remains subject to its original GPL license, included
with each software snapshot. Its embedded Flexiplex license is preserved with
the LR-Split-seq source.

The FLAMES figure-analysis and LongBench workflow repositories did not supply
a root license at the preserved commits. Their authors retain their copyrights;
this archive does not apply the RAD manuscript repository's MIT license to
those files. Their original author, repository, commit and path metadata are
preserved in `origins.json`. Execution artifacts are identified separately
from original upstream files.
