# Data availability audit: benchmark and spatial inputs

Verified 22 October 2026. Scope: retained LongBench, ScMixology2, ScTagger S/L and Visium HD inputs. This is a partial source map for integration with the other 37-input dataset families; it excludes removed Figure 8/NBC-only work. No deposit was created or updated.

## Local linkage and important exclusions

- Inventory: `/Volumes/rad_data/reviewer_comment_data/three_tool_whitelist_complete_rageseq_immune_20260901/sample_inventory.tsv`, lines 2–3 (ScTagger S/L), 10–13 (four LongBench inputs), 38 (ScMixology2 reconstruction), 39 (Visium HD, with four flow-cell IDs). Visium HD is an inventory parent and is not an additional scalar three-tool whitelist input.
- LongBench download linkage: `/Volumes/rad_data/reviewer_comment_data/scratch_preservation_20260921/files/rad_longbench_20260726/data/sc_sn/scripts/download_sc_sn.sh` constructs downloads from `https://longbench-data.s3.amazonaws.com/` and the saved object inventory.
- Bulk donor panel: `/Volumes/rad_data/reviewer_comment_data/LongBench_comps/SN_ONT_RAD_bulk_cellline_bundle_20260803/bulk_donor_panel/provenance/README.md`, lines 3–6 and 18–26 document reconstruction from eight separate bulk Illumina libraries; lines 47–61 distinguish this reconstruction from the unavailable authors' private donor VCF. Its public deposition has not been verified.
- ScMixology2 used files: `/Volumes/rad_data/reviewer_comment_data/blaze_single_cell_expect_sweep_20260810/exploratory/scmixology2/evidence/local_source_inventory.tsv`, lines 2–3: `data/scmixology/blaze/raw_bc.csv.gz`, 684,400,925 bytes, SHA256 `35175dd838d5ade27db2e009b8786c2c71dc9fadbdaea1fc53aba388ffd589f7`, 25,164,948 data rows; `data/scmixology/cellranger/whitelist.tsv`, 4,712 bytes, SHA256 `55dab619f152ad60635138d8c15a5c1e695a27d97847bcb3a159d1a3a31e41a1`, 248 unique normalized barcodes. `scripts/run.sh` lines 7–8 consumes those two files, and `provenance.json` lines 14–26 records the same sources and count checks. The RAD scan provenance separately records its local input as `raw_fastq_datasets/scmix2.fq.gz`; this audit did not perform byte-level matching of that large local FASTQ to ENA.
- Do not include GSE142285 merely because the original FLAMES paper groups it with ScMixology2. Official GEO metadata identifies GSE142285 as the associated scATAC-seq validation dataset. No retained RAD input requiring that ATAC dataset was identified.
- Do not use the adjacent Tian-study accession/run SRR12282458 as the ScMixology2 source. The exact relevant BLAZE rebasecall is ERR9958136; the original RNA series is GSE154870.
- Native quality derivation: `/Volumes/rad_data/reviewer_comment_data/scratch_preservation_20260921/files/sctagger_nanopore2023_unadjusted_20260913/README.md`, lines 9–32, identifies the generated native FASTQ and unchanged sequence/header contract. The earlier nanopore2018 and uniform-Q20 inputs are also derived forms of the source simulation.
- Sweep outputs: `/Volumes/rad_data/reviewer_comment_data/scratch_preservation_20260921/files/sctagger_nanopore2023_extended_sweep_20260913/README.md`, lines 3–7 and 53–78, documents the eleven shifted conditions as whitelist replay, reused quality-invariant callsets, quality histograms and result tables. Do not promise eleven full shifted FASTQs: those replay conditions were not all materialized as new full FASTQ files.

## Suggested wording fragment for integration

LongBench single-cell and single-nucleus ONT and PacBio reads, matched Illumina reads, and the eight cell-line bulk Illumina libraries are available under GEO accession GSE303762 and BioProject PRJNA1171488 and through the LongBench data portal. ScMixology2 matched RNA data are available under GSE154870. The rebasecalled ScMixology2 Nanopore reads are available under ENA accession ERR9958136 within PRJEB54718. The published BLAZE barcode table and matched Cell Ranger reference used for the ScMixology2 reconstruction are available at DOI 10.26188/20335203.v1. The original ScTagger synthetic datasets are available at DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.19740475.v1. The four-flow-cell Visium HD 3′ mouse-brain dataset and accompanying spatial outputs are available from the ONT Open Data release `visium_hd_2025.06`.

Derived uniform-Q20 and model-resampled simulation data, quality-sweep tables and callsets, the reconstructed LongBench bulk donor panel, and the RAD-study source data require a final public repository accession/link. No current public deposit for these derived materials was verified. The root audit found that the manuscript repository and reserved Zenodo draft are private; do not present either as an accessible published deposit.
